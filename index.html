● AI memorial, based on your memories

Her voice. Her stories. Kept with love.

Echoes has two phases. While she's here, she fills in her own questions and records her voice. Only when you choose to open the memorial does it become a voice conversation you can return to.

Phase 1 · While she's here

Capture, with her permission

  • She reads and agrees to a clear consent page.
  • She fills in the questions herself — foods, sayings, stories, routine.
  • She records a short voice sample in her own words.
  • Everything stays locked away until you need it.
Phase 2 · When the time comes

Talk, with eyes open

  • You deliberately switch the memorial on.
  • Voice conversations use her sample — clearly labelled as AI every time.
  • Crisis language is caught before the AI replies.
  • You can delete the voice and every answer in one action.

Just want to see it?

Try a complete sample memorial — Margaret, her answers, varied replies, a simulated sample and a browser demo voice. Nothing to fill in, nothing saved.

What has to be true for this to be okay

  • She consents while she is alive, knowing exactly what is being made and that it will speak after her death.
  • It never pretends to be her. A permanent "AI memorial" badge shows in text and speech.
  • No raw voice is downloadable or exported. The sample stays with the provider only.
  • She can withdraw and have everything deleted at any time.
