Phase 1 · While they're here

Capture, with their permission

  • They read and agree to a clear consent page.
  • They fill in the questions themselves — foods, sayings, stories, routine.
  • They record a short voice sample in their own words.
  • Everything stays locked away until you need it.
Phase 2 · When the time comes

Talk, with eyes open

  • You deliberately switch the memorial on.
  • Voice conversations use their sample — clearly labelled as AI every time.
  • Crisis language is caught before the AI replies.
  • You can delete the voice and every answer in one action.

What has to be true for this to be okay

  • They consent while they are alive, knowing exactly what is being made and that it will speak after they die.
